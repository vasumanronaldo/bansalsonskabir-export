---
description: Write or rewrite copy in the house voice
---
Read `docs/01-brand.md`, section Voice. Nothing else.

Write the requested copy. Then self-check every sentence:
- Is there a concrete noun, number or verifiable fact in it? If not, cut or rewrite.
- Could a competitor put their name on this sentence unchanged? If yes, it is too generic.
- Does it sell, urge, or flatter? Remove it.
- Sentence case. Indian English. "jewellery", not "jewelry".

Give one option, not three. Then note in one line what you would need from the
client to make it more specific.

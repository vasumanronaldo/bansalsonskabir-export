# 01 — Brand

## Positioning

Luxury defined by **trust, not price**. The house competes on integrity,
manufacturing control and lifelong relationships — never on discount or urgency.

The site is not a catalogue with a story attached. It is an argument for
trustworthiness, evidenced by craft, that ends in a private appointment.

## The five proofs (every page should advance at least one)

1. **Manufacturer, not reseller** — the piece is made under this roof, start to finish.
2. **Certification** — natural GIA/IGI diamonds only, BIS hallmarked, HUID.
3. **Continuity** — one family, three generations, same address, since 1993.
4. **Transparency** — billing and packing done in front of the client; treatments disclosed in writing.
5. **Aftercare** — lifetime service on anything the house has made.

## Voice

**Write like this**
- "Every piece is made in our own workshop. Nothing is bought in and rebranded."
- "If a stone has been treated, we tell you before it is set, in writing."
- "Billing and packing happen in front of you. You see what goes into the box."

**Never like this**
- "Exquisite craftsmanship that redefines luxury" (superlative, says nothing)
- "Your dream jewellery awaits" (sales copy)
- "Unparalleled elegance" (empty)
- "Book now!" / "Don't miss out" (urgency)

**Rules**
- Sentence case for headings. No Title Case, no ALL CAPS except small mono labels.
- Prefer the concrete noun to the abstract one: *claw, casting, loupe, ledger, karat*
  over *elegance, sophistication, opulence*.
- Numbers over adjectives. "Three to five months" beats "meticulously crafted over time".
- Say "we", not "Bansal Sons Jewellers strives to".
- Indian English. "Jewellery", not "jewelry". Rupee amounts never shown.

## Customer fears the copy must defuse

| Fear | Answer on the site |
|---|---|
| Fake diamonds / low purity | GIA & IGI certificates, BIS hallmark + HUID, explained plainly on the Craftsmanship page |
| Hidden charges | A published, itemised explanation of how a price is built (making charges, wastage, stone value, GST) |
| Pushy salespeople | "Appointment-first. We will not call you twice." Stated in the appointment flow |
| Poor resale | Buyback and exchange policy stated in writing |
| Not being respected | Owner greets clients; private cabin available; walk-ins welcomed |

## Audience

Bridal families, business families, professionals, NRIs, multi-generational
clients. Skews **mobile, evening, Delhi NCR**. Many arrive after a personal
referral and are verifying, not discovering. Design for the sceptical verifier.

## Values (do not list these on the site)

Trust, transparency, craftsmanship, relationships, honesty, personal attention,
timeless design, excellence, heritage, integrity, resilience, responsibility,
quality control.

A wall of thirteen abstract nouns is the weakest possible use of a page.
**Demonstrate them; never enumerate them.** If a section is tempted to list
values, replace it with a specific fact that proves one.

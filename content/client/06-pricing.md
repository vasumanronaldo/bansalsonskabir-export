# How a price is built

Every bill we issue is itemised. Before you decide anything, you are told which
of the following applies and what it comes to.

**Metal value** — the weight of gold or platinum in the piece, multiplied by the
day's rate. The rate we use is the published bullion rate for that morning. Weight
is taken in front of you.

**Making charges** — the labour of the bench. Charged as a percentage of metal
value, varying by how the piece is made: machine-made chain at the low end,
hand-set jadau and polki at the high end.

- Machine-made and light gold: `[TK]%`
- Standard handmade: `[TK]%`
- Polki, kundan and jadau: `[TK]%`
- Bespoke commissions: quoted per piece at the sketch stage

**Stone value** — quoted separately and per stone, with the certificate attached.
Where a stone has been treated, that is stated in writing before it is set.

**Wastage** — `[TK]`. State the policy plainly here, including whether it is
charged at all on which categories.

**GST** — charged at the prevailing rate on the invoice total, shown as a
separate line.

## What we do not do

We do not quote a single all-in figure and leave you to guess what is inside it.
We do not change the rate between the quote and the bill. We do not charge for
the sketch, the CAD render, or the consultation.

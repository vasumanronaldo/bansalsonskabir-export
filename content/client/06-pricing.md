# How a price is built

Every bill we issue is itemised. Before you decide anything, you are told which of
the following applies and what it comes to.

**Metal value** — the weight of gold or platinum in the piece, multiplied by the
bullion rate at the moment. The rate we use is the published bullion rate at the
time of billing. Weight is taken in front of you.

**Making charges** — the labour of the bench. These vary according to how the
piece is made, reflecting the time, skill and craftsmanship involved — from
machine-made chain and lighter work to hand-set jadau and polki.

- Machine-made and light gold
- Standard handmade
- Polki, kundan and jadau
- Bespoke commissions: quoted per piece at the sketch stage

**Stone value** — quoted separately and per stone, with the certificate attached.
Where a stone has been treated, that is stated in writing before it is set.

**Wastage** — the unavoidable material loss that occurs during the manufacturing
process. Gold and other precious metals are cut, filed, shaped, soldered and
polished at the bench, and small amounts can be lost as dust, filings, fragments
and material removed during finishing. Wastage reflects this genuine manufacturing
loss and is charged transparently rather than being concealed within the price of
the piece.

**GST** — charged at the prevailing rate on the invoice total, shown as a separate
line.

## What we do not do

We do not quote a single all-in figure and leave you to guess what is inside it.
We do not change the rate between the quote and the bill. We do not charge for the
sketch, the CAD render, or the consultation.

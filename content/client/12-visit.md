# What a visit is like

Greeted by a member of the family, made comfortable, then introduced to each
piece — one at a time, with the attention it deserves.

We tell you what you are seeing, how it was made, and what it costs.

The piece is weighed, billed and presented to you at the table.

And before it leaves our hands, it receives a blessing.

## The room

Grey Italian marble underfoot, black marble on the feature walls, and light from above. Nine seating areas, so a conversation is never overheard, and one private cabin for the occasions that call for it. Nothing is behind glass you cannot ask to hold.

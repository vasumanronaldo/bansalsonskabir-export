# Privacy

Short version: we collect only what we need to reply to you, we do not sell it,
and we do not share it.

## What the appointment form collects

Your name, phone number, and — if you choose to give them — your email address,
preferred date and time, the occasion, what you are interested in, an optional
budget range, and anything you write in the requirement box.

## What happens to it

It is emailed to us and stored in our content system so we can prepare for your
visit. It is visible to the family and to the two members of staff who manage
appointments. Nothing more.

## How long we keep it

12 months after your appointment, or until you ask us to delete it, whichever
is sooner.

## What we do not do

We do not sell, rent or share your details with anyone. We do not add you to a
marketing list unless you separately ask for the journal. We do not use tracking
cookies or advertising pixels; our analytics are cookieless and count page views
without identifying you.

## Contacting you

We contact you once to confirm the appointment and once on the day. We do not
call after that.

## Deleting your details

Write to bansalsonsjewellers18@gmail.com or call +91 85272 92840 and we will
delete everything we hold. No reason is needed.

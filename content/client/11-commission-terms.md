# Commissioning a piece — the terms, before you come in

Most houses explain a commission emotionally and leave the terms until you are
already sitting down. We would rather you read them first.

## What it costs to start

Nothing. The consultation, the sketch and the CAD render are free, and remain
free if you decide not to proceed.

## The deposit

`[TK]%` of the quoted total, taken once you approve the design and we begin
buying metal and stones. It is credited against the final bill.

## Changes

`[TK]` rounds of revision to the sketch at no charge. Changes after casting has
begun are quoted separately, because the metal has already been committed.

## Cancellation

Before casting: the deposit is returned in full, less the cost of any stones
already bought to your specification. After casting: `[TK]`.

## Delivery

We quote a completion date at the sketch stage and hold to it within `[TK]`.
If we are going to be late, you hear it from us before the date, not after.

## If it is wrong

If the finished piece does not match the approved design, we remake it. Not
adjust, not compensate — remake, at our cost. `[TK]` — state the exact standard.

## Who owns the design

`[TK]`. State plainly whether a bespoke design may be made again for someone
else, and whether the client can take the drawing elsewhere.

## What we will not make

We will say no to a commission if the design will not survive being worn, if the
stone is wrong for the setting, or if we do not believe we are the right house
for it. You will be told why, and told who might be better placed.

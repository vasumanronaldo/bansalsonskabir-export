# Commissioning a piece — the terms, before you come in

Most houses explain a commission emotionally and leave the terms until you are
already sitting down. We would rather you read them first.

## What it costs to start

Nothing. The consultation, the sketch and the CAD render are free, and remain free
if you decide not to proceed.

## The deposit

30% of the quoted total, taken once you approve the design and we begin buying
metal and stones. It is credited against the final bill.

No advances are required if you are a regular.

## Changes

Changes to the design are included at no additional charge, whether made at the
sketch stage or after work has begun. We believe the piece should be right before
it is yours — so there are no charges for revisions.

## Cancellation

Before casting: the deposit is returned in full, less the cost of any stones
already bought to your specification.

## Delivery

We quote a completion date at the sketch stage and hold to it within the two to
four week window. If we are going to be late, you hear it from us before the date,
not after.

## Who owns the design

One vision. One creation. Entirely yours.

## What we will not make

We will say no to a commission if the design will not survive being worn, if the
stone is wrong for the setting, or if we do not believe we are the right house for
it. You will be told why, and told who might be better placed.
